Scan anything into your iPhone: an ID, a prescription, a receipt, a contract, a page of lab results, or the number someone wrote on a napkin.

FAST
- Instant Scan: one press of the Action button, a Control Center or Lock Screen button, or "Scan with Pocket Scanner" to Siri opens the camera
- Apple's document scanner finds the edges, straightens the page and captures several pages in one go
- Every scan is saved as a searchable PDF and named for you; rename it whenever you like

ORGANIZED YOUR WAY
- Folders inside folders, like Medical > Lab Results or Taxes > 2025
- Move documents between folders, sort by date, name or page count, and switch between a list and a grid
- Search your whole library by name or by the text inside a document; matches are highlighted right on the page

SIGN AND MARK UP
- Scan your signature once, then place it and the date on any document. No printer needed
- Highlight or strike through text, crop, rotate and filter pages, and split pages out into a new document
- Import PDFs you already have

YOURS
- App Lock: Face ID keeps the library private, and the app switcher hides your pages
- iCloud Drive keeps scans on all your devices, in your own storage. Or use the app without iCloud and keep everything on this iPhone
- Choose the look of new scans (Color, Greyscale, Black & White or Photo) and the page size of the PDF

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO

- Doesn't collect any data about you. None. No analytics, no telemetry, no behavioural tracking.
- Doesn't have a "Pro" tier. Every feature is included.
- Doesn't store anything on our servers. Your documents go from your camera to your iCloud account, full stop.
- Doesn't show ads.

PRIVACY

Pocket Scanner doesn't have a server. Your scans never leave your device except to sync to your own iCloud account (Apple's storage you already pay for, or the free tier). The OCR that makes your documents searchable runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. We don't see your documents, your names, your filenames, your search queries, or your usage patterns.

Full privacy policy: https://peterajones.github.io/PocketScanner/privacy-policy

REQUIREMENTS

- iOS 17.6 or later
- Control Center and Lock Screen buttons need iOS 18 or later; the Action button needs an iPhone that has one
- iCloud Drive recommended (works in local-only mode if you prefer)

ABOUT

Built by one independent developer who got tired of every scanner app demanding a subscription. No subscription, no ads, no account. Pay once, scan forever. If you like the app, a review is the most helpful thing you can leave.
