Instant Scan: one press opens the camera, from anywhere on your iPhone. The scan is saved, made searchable and opened for you. Rename it later if you like.

Ways to use it:
- Action button: Settings > Action Button > Controls > Scan Document
- Control Center: tap + and add Scan Document
- Lock Screen: remove one of the two bottom buttons, then add Scan Document
- Siri or Spotlight: "Scan with Pocket Scanner" (the first time, Siri asks to turn on the app's shortcuts)

With App Lock on, the camera opens first and Face ID comes before anything in your library is shown.

As always: no subscriptions, no ads, no tracking, nothing stored on our servers. Pay once, scan forever. Thanks for scanning with Pocket Scanner.
