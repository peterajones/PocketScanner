New: an eraser for scanned pages. Get rid of staple holes, specks along the edges and handwritten notes you no longer need.

Open a document, tap Edit Pages, tap a page, then switch to Erase. Drag a box over anything you want gone; Undo removes the last box. Tap Apply and it's removed, and erased words no longer show up in search.

As always: no subscriptions, no ads, no tracking, nothing stored on our servers. Pay once, scan forever. Thanks for scanning with Pocket Scanner.
