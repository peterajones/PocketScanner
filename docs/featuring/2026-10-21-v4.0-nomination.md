## Description (≤ 1,000)

```text
Pocket Scanner 4.0 adds Instant Scan: one press opens the document camera from anywhere.

It is built on App Intents. A single Scan Document intent drives a Control (Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action button, through WidgetKit Controls on iOS 18), an App Shortcut ("Scan with Pocket Scanner") for Siri and Spotlight, and the Shortcuts app. After the scan, the document is saved with on-device OCR, named automatically and opened in the library. With the app's Face ID lock on, the camera opens first, the scan is saved, and nothing in the library is shown until Face ID succeeds.

The goal is the moment when paper is in front of you and you have seconds: a prescription left on the table, a receipt at the counter, a phone number on a napkin.

Pocket Scanner is a one-time purchase with no subscription, no ads, no account and no server. Scans stay on the device or in the user's own iCloud Drive. Localized in English, French, Spanish, German, Italian and Simplified Chinese.
```
